MedBuddy Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information the MedBuddy Android app (package com.oshadharonlab.medbuddy) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. MedBuddy is developed by oshadharonlab ("we", "us").

MedBuddy does not show ads, does not include third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, does not access your location, contacts, camera or files, and does not sell or share your personal data.

Information we collect

DataWhyWhere it is kept
Name, email address and password To create your account and sign you in Our server. Passwords are stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text.
Sign-in tokens To keep you signed in Your device and our server, until you sign out or they expire
Medicine search text To return matching medicines Sent to our server to answer the search. Recent results are cached on your device for up to 7 days.
Search cache statistics and a random app-install ID To measure how well search caching works and improve performance Our server, linked to your account. Only daily counts of cached and uncached searches are sent, not your search text. The ID is randomly generated by the app and is not a hardware or advertising identifier.
Cart, saved drafts, My Meds and settings To run the app's features Only on your device. This data is not uploaded to our server.

How we use information

  • Providing, maintaining and securing your account and the app's features.
  • Improving search speed and reliability.
  • Responding to your support or deletion requests.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with third parties. Our server and database run on cloud hosting providers that store and process data on our behalf only to operate the service. We may disclose information if required by law.

Security

All communication between the app and our server uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed, and access to the server and database is restricted. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

Retention

Account information and search cache statistics are kept while your account exists. Sign-in tokens expire automatically. When your account is deleted, your account data, tokens and statistics are deleted from our server.

Deleting your account and data

To delete your MedBuddy account and all associated data, email oshadharondev@gmail.com from the email address you registered with, using the subject "MedBuddy account deletion". We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Data stored only on your device (cart, drafts, My Meds, settings and cached searches) is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android settings.

Children

MedBuddy is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Medical disclaimer

Medicine information and prices in MedBuddy are for reference only and may not be current. MedBuddy does not provide medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
