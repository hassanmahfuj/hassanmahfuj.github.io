Hishab Kitab Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Hishab Kitab (com.oshadharonlab.hk) is a personal income and expense tracking app published by Oshadharon Lab ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

DataWhy we use it
Name, email address and passwordTo create your account and sign you in. Your password is handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it.
Address (optional)Shown on your profile if you choose to add it.
Your financial records: account names, categories, income, expense and transfer entries, amounts, notes and datesThis is the core of the app. We store it so you can see your balances and history on any device you sign in to.

Information collected automatically

  • Account activity: the date you joined and when you last opened the app.
  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): which screens are used, app version, device model, operating system version, country and an app instance identifier. We use this to understand how the app is used and to improve it.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): technical details when the app crashes, such as the error, device model, OS version and an installation identifier. We use this only to find and fix bugs.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, SMS or call logs, and the app shows no ads.

Where your data is stored

Your account and records are stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), operated by Google LLC, and may be processed on servers outside your country. A copy is also kept on your device so the app works offline; it syncs when you are back online. Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, and only your signed-in account can read or change your records.

Google's handling of this data is described in the Firebase privacy and security page and the Google Privacy Policy.

PDF reports

When you export a report, the PDF is created on your device and saved to your Downloads folder. It is not uploaded anywhere by us. On Android 9 and older, the app asks for storage permission only for this purpose.

How we share information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only with the service providers listed above (Google Firebase) so the app can work, or when required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your records stay in your account until you delete them. You can delete individual entries, all entries of an account, accounts and categories at any time from inside the app.
  • To delete your whole Hishab Kitab account and all data linked to it, email oshadharondev@gmail.com from the email address on your account, or send us a request through Messenger. We will delete it within 30 days.
  • Analytics and crash data are kept for a limited period under Firebase's retention settings and then deleted automatically.

Children

Hishab Kitab is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top will show when it was last updated.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: email oshadharondev@gmail.com or message us on Messenger.
