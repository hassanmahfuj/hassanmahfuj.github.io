Salat Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Salat (com.oshadharonlab.salat) is a prayer times app published by Oshadharon Lab ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

Salat has no account, no ads, no analytics and no servers of its own. Everything the app needs is calculated and stored on your device. We do not collect, receive or store any of your personal information.

Location

Prayer times depend on where you are, so the app asks for permission to access your device's location (precise or approximate).

  • Your coordinates are used on your device to calculate prayer times, haram windows and Salatut Duha timing.
  • To show your city and country name, the coordinates are passed to your device's built-in geocoding service. On most Android phones this service is provided by Google, and its handling of the request is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Your last known coordinates, city and country are saved on your device so prayer times load instantly and work offline. They are never sent to us.

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your device settings. The app will then be unable to calculate prayer times for a new location.

Settings stored on your device

Your chosen calculation method, madhab, theme and language are saved locally on your device so the app remembers them. They are not shared with anyone.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, contacts, photos, files, SMS, call logs or device identifiers. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting tools.

How we share information

Because we do not collect your information, we have nothing to sell, rent or share.

Deleting your data

All data the app keeps is on your device. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children

Salat does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top will show when it was last updated.
