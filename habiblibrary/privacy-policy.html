Privacy Policy

Habib Library for Android · Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Habib Library app collects, how it is used and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Habib Library is a book catalogue. You do not need an account, and the app does not ask for your name, mobile number, email, address or any other personal details. The app only reads the book list, categories and banners from our servers; it never sends your searches or anything you do in the app back to us.

Like any internet service, the servers that deliver the catalogue and book images automatically receive technical details needed to answer each request, such as your IP address and device type. We do not use these details to identify you.

The app stores the catalogue and images it has already loaded on your device so it opens faster and works offline. This cache stays on your device and is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Permissions

  • Internet, to load books, categories, banners and images.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone or files.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, which hosts the book catalogue.
  • Cloudflare R2, which hosts book and banner images.
  • Google Play, which installs and updates the app.

These providers handle request data under their own privacy policies.

We do not sell information, show advertising or use analytics or tracking in the app.

Accounts from earlier versions

Versions of the app before 8.0 let you sign in with your mobile number, save a profile and place orders. Version 8.0 and later no longer collect this information. Data saved by earlier versions (mobile number, profile details and photo, cart and order records) remains stored in Firebase. To have it deleted, contact us using the details below from your registered mobile number. We will delete it within 30 days, except order records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

Security

All data is sent over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect the information we hold.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with a new "Last updated" date.
