Privacy Policy

Habib Library for Android · Last updated 16 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Habib Library app collects, how it is used and what choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

  • Mobile number. You sign in with your mobile number, which is verified with a one-time SMS code.
  • Profile details. Name, email, shipping address and profile photo, if you choose to add them.
  • Cart and orders. Books in your cart, and for each order: the items, prices, order date, delivery method, payment method and, for home delivery, the recipient's name, mobile number and address.
  • Search history. Your recent searches are saved only on your device.
  • Usage counts. We count app visits per day and how often each search term is used. These counts are anonymous and are not linked to you or your account.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or payment card or bKash account details. If you choose bKash, you pay in the bKash app; Habib Library records only that bKash was selected.

Permissions

  • Internet to load books and save your cart, profile and orders.
  • Photos, only when you tap "Upload Image" on your profile and pick a photo yourself.

How we use your information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To process, deliver and support your orders, including contacting you about an order.
  • To show your cart, order history and profile across sessions.
  • To understand which books people look for and improve our catalogue, using the anonymous counts only.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Services we use

Your information is stored and processed by Google services that the app is built on:

  • Firebase Authentication, Realtime Database and Cloud Storage, for sign-in, account and order data, and profile photos.
  • Google Play services, to deliver app updates.

The "Special sim offers" page can open Facebook Messenger. Anything you send there is covered by Meta's privacy policy.

Sharing

Your order and delivery details are seen only by Habib Library staff who handle orders, and by delivery partners as needed to deliver them. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your account, profile and order records for as long as your account exists, so you can see your order history. You can edit your profile in the app at any time. To delete your account and the data linked to it, contact us using the details below from your registered mobile number. We will delete it within 30 days, except order records we must keep for legal or accounting reasons.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored on Google's infrastructure. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page, with a new "Last updated" date.
